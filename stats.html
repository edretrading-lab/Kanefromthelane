The story

The long way to the top

Nobody handed him anything. He went on loan four times. He played midfield as a kid. He got good by refusing to stop getting better.

  1. 1999

    Ridgeway Rovers, age six

    A local Walthamstow club. He grew up fifteen minutes from White Hart Lane. His hero was Teddy Sheringham.

  2. 2002

    Released by Arsenal

    One season in their academy, then out. Arsène Wenger later admitted it was a mistake. A Watford trial got him a second look from Spurs.

  3. 2011–13

    Four loans, one lesson

    Leyton Orient, Millwall, Norwich, Leicester. A broken foot at Norwich. Millwall's Young Player of the Year. He learned the trade the hard way.

  4. 2014

    The goal that saved a manager

    A 90th-minute winner against Aston Villa. Mauricio Pochettino later said it kept him in the job. Kane never left the team again.

  5. 2015

    Eighty seconds

    His England debut against Lithuania. He came off the bench for Wayne Rooney and scored with his first real touch.

  6. 2018

    World Cup Golden Boot

    Six goals in Russia as captain. The first Englishman to win it since Gary Lineker in 1986. He was made an MBE that winter.

  7. 2023

    Spurs' greatest ever, then goodbye

    He passed Jimmy Greaves to become the club's all-time top scorer. Then Bayern paid a Bundesliga record of up to €110 million.

  8. 2025

    Finally, a trophy

    The Bundesliga title. His first major honour, at 31. He was also voted the league's Player of the Season.

  9. 2026

    The season of seventy

    A league and cup double. A hat-trick in the cup final. 61 goals for Bayern and 73 in all. Only Messi has scored more in one campaign.

  10. Oct 2026

    The Ballon d'Or

    The best player in the world. The first Englishman to win it since Michael Owen in 2001. The boy Arsenal let go is now the best player in the world.

Off the pitch

The man

He married Katie Goodland, who he has known since school. They have four children. Their two Labradors are called Brady and Wilson. One is named after Tom Brady. The other only sounds like it.

He skips alcohol in season. He has had a full-time chef since 2017. He plays golf off a 3 handicap. He once said he might try kicking in the NFL. In 2020 he paid for Leyton Orient's shirt sponsorship and gave it to charity.

His foundation works on mental health. There is a statue of him in Walthamstow.

Quick answers

Your questions

Straight answers, kept up to date.

How many goals has Harry Kane scored for England?

93 goals in 127 games. That makes him England's all-time top scorer, well clear of Wayne Rooney's 53. He is also England's most-capped player.

How many goals has Harry Kane scored in his career?

545 goals in 789 games for club and country, counting Tottenham, his four loan clubs, Bayern Munich and England. He became the first Englishman to pass 500 professional goals.

How many goals has Kane scored this season?

In the 2026/27 Bundesliga he has 4 goals and 8 assists in 9 games. The numbers on this page are refreshed every week.

How many goals has Harry Kane scored for Bayern Munich?

156 goals in 162 games since joining from Tottenham in August 2023. He reached 100 for the club in just 104 games.

How many World Cup goals has Harry Kane scored?

14. That is an England record. He passed Gary Lineker's 10 at the 2026 World Cup and now sits joint fifth of all time with Gerd Müller, two ahead of Pelé.

Has Harry Kane won a trophy?

Yes. He won the Bundesliga with Bayern in 2025. In 2025/26 he won the league and DFB-Pokal double, scoring a hat-trick in the cup final.

Has Harry Kane won the Ballon d'Or?

Yes. He won the 2026 Ballon d'Or, the first Englishman to win it since Michael Owen in 2001.

How old is Harry Kane and when does his contract end?

He was born on 28 July 1993 in Leytonstone, London, so he is 33. His Bayern Munich contract runs to June 2029.

How many goals did Kane score for Tottenham?

280 in 435 games. He is Spurs' all-time top scorer, having passed Jimmy Greaves' 266 in 2023. 213 of those came in the Premier League.

How many Golden Boots has Harry Kane won?

Three Premier League Golden Boots (2015/16, 2016/17 and 2020/21). The 2018 World Cup Golden Boot. Three Bundesliga top-scorer titles in a row. Two European Golden Shoes.

Can Harry Kane beat Lewandowski's 41-goal Bundesliga record?

Robert Lewandowski scored 41 Bundesliga goals in 2020/21. Kane's best is 36, which he has done twice. This season he has 4 league goals so far.

Who has more goals, Kane or Haaland?

Kane, by a long way in total, because he is seven years older. Haaland scores more often per game. See the full Kane vs Haaland comparison.
