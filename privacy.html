Privacy policy

Your privacy

Last updated 19 November 2026.

Who we are

Kane From The Lane (kanefromthelane.com) is published by Aqueous International Ltd. Our registered address is Rm B, 6/F, Real Sight Commercial Building, 122 Woosung Street, Jordan, Kowloon, Hong Kong. You can reach us through our contact page.

What we collect

Goal alerts. If you sign up, we keep your first name and email address. We use them only to send you goal alerts, record updates and the occasional Kane news email.

Contact messages. If you message us, we keep your name, email and message so we can reply.

Visits. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It does not use cookies and does not track you across other websites. It tells us things like how many people visited, which pages they read and which country they came from.

Who handles your data

The site is hosted by Netlify, which also receives our form submissions. Our email list is run by an email service provider such as MailerLite. These companies process data for us under their own privacy terms. Some of them are based outside the UK and EU. They use standard legal safeguards for international transfers.

Adverts

We may show adverts from Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads, including ads based on your visits to this and other sites. If you are in the UK, the EU or Switzerland, we will ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time. Read how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can turn off personalised ads at adssettings.google.com.

Affiliate links

Some links to shops, such as shirt retailers, are affiliate links. If you buy something, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. The shop may set its own cookies when you click through.

Your rights

You can unsubscribe from our emails at any time using the link in every email. You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the data we hold about you. If you are in the UK or EU you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address until you unsubscribe. We keep contact messages for up to two years.

Children

Goal alerts are meant for people aged 16 and over.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Kane From The Lane is an unofficial fan site. Not affiliated with Harry Kane, FC Bayern Munich, Tottenham Hotspur or the FA.
