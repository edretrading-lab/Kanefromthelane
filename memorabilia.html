Avoid the fakes

Seven checks before you buy

  • A certificate on its own is not enoughAnyone can print one. Look for a well-known company behind it and a way to check it online.
  • Photo proofThe best sellers show a photo or video of Kane signing that exact item.
  • Match-worn means photo-matchedFor match-worn shirts, ask for photos matching marks, creases or numbering to a specific game.
  • Compare the signatureLook at verified Kane signatures. Fakes are often too neat, too slow or the wrong shape.
  • Too cheap is a warningA signed Kane shirt for £40 is almost certainly fake.
  • Check the seller's historyReviews, years trading and a real business address.
  • Pay safelyUse a card or a platform with buyer protection. Never pay by bank transfer to a stranger.

Quick answers

Collector questions

Straight answers, kept up to date.

How do I know a signed Harry Kane shirt is real?

Buy from a trusted company that shows photo or video proof of the signing, with a certificate you can check online. Be wary of very cheap items and sellers with no history.

Where can I buy a signed Harry Kane shirt?

Icons.com is the best-known specialist. Rarer items appear at auction on Catawiki and at Graham Budd Auctions.

What is a match-worn shirt?

A shirt Kane actually wore in a game. Genuine ones can be photo-matched to that match and cost far more than signed replica shirts.
