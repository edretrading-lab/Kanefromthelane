Kane v Haaland

Quick answers

Straight answers, kept up to date.

Who has more goals, Kane or Haaland?

Kane. He has 545 goals for club and country to Haaland's 377. Haaland is seven years younger though. He also scores more often: 0.87 goals a game to Kane's 0.69.

Who is better, Kane or Haaland?

It depends what you value. Haaland is the purer finisher and scores more per game. Kane creates far more for team-mates and won the 2026 Ballon d'Or. This season Kane has more goals and assists combined in the league.

Who scored more at the 2026 World Cup, Kane or Haaland?

Haaland, with 7 goals to Kane's 6. But England knocked Norway out 2-1 after extra time in the quarter-final, with Jude Bellingham scoring both. England went on to finish third.

How old are Kane and Haaland?

Harry Kane was born on 28 July 1993, so he is 33. Erling Haaland was born on 21 July 2000, so he is 26.

Who has scored more international goals?

Kane, with 93 for England in 127 games. Haaland has 68 for Norway in 61 games, a better rate per game. Both are their country's all-time top scorer.
